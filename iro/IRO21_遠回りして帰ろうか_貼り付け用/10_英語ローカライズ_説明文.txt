Taking the Long Way Home | 2 Hours of Night Drive Chill Music

City lights across the bay. In the passenger seat, a big golden retriever has already drifted off.
Two hours for a night that is a little too nice to head straight home.

Pedal steel, clarinet, steel pan, fretless bass, oboe and Hammond organ drift over an easy, unhurried groove.

A quiet companion for night drives, the way home and calm work, without a timer.
