I Only Meant to Wait Out the Rain | Café Jazz Lo-fi 2 Hours

A sudden shower, and a small café on the corner.
She only meant to stay a little while — and stayed until closing.

From a rainy afternoon, through the evening after the rain, into the lamp-lit night:
two hours of café jazz lo-fi at a seat by the window.

Accordion, ukulele, music box, bandoneon, charango and alto recorder,
over soft brushed drums and upright bass — the tracks rotate between different lead instruments.
No track repeats in the full two hours. No vocals, no rain sounds.

At her feet, a golden retriever sleeps the whole time.
