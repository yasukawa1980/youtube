Spread out your yarn and take it one stitch at a time. Two hours of acoustic chill for autumn and winter crafts, in a warm room with a sleeping dog.

Soft felt piano, wooden marimba, a quiet string ensemble, low tenor saxophone, mellow vibraphone and distant French horn move gently from one texture to the next.

A woman knits slowly, stitch by stitch, while a golden retriever sleeps curled up on the rug. A quiet companion for knitting, reading and everyday work, without a timer.
