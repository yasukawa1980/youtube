## タイトル

Did Washington Really Cross the Delaware Like This?

## 説明欄

A winter night in 1776, a flag adopted in 1777, and a painting made about seventy-five years after the crossing. Follow Emanuel Leutze’s Washington Crossing the Delaware from the river to a studio in Düsseldorf and an exhibition in New York.
