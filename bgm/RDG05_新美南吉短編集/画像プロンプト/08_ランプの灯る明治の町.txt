A Japanese port town street at dusk in the late Meiji era, about 1900, drawn as an evening scene. A row of large merchant shops with deep eaves and open fronts lines the street, and every shop is lit by many glass kerosene lamps hanging under the eaves, glowing like flowers, so the whole street shines warm and bright as a dream palace. Inside one shop, bolts of cloth printed with big camellia flowers lie spread under the lamps; another shop has dozens of lamps of every shape hanging for sale. The sky above the roofs is deep blue twilight. No electric poles, no wires. Colours: warm amber lamplight, deep blue sky, dark wooden shopfronts, a touch of camellia red. Enchanted and nostalgic.
