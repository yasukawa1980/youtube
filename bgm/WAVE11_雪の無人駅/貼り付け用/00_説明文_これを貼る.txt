A tiny unmanned station on a single mountain line in the snow country of Japan, on an early-winter night: from the last snowfall at dusk, through the hour the lamp comes on and the hush after the snow has stopped, to the faint glow of the snow at midnight and the deep blue before dawn. One platform lamp and one small waiting-room window are the only lights. The eleventh volume of Waves of Japan.

This three-hour original ambient piece combines soft pads, felt-piano-like notes, quiet plucked tones, and small rounded points of sound. The melodic notes are drawn only from the five tones of the Japanese in scale (E F A B D). The music was synthesized locally from original parameters without Suno, third-party songs, or sampled recordings. There are no train or environmental sounds in the audio.

A subtle stereo layer holds a tone in each channel around 146.83Hz (D3). The channel difference descends slowly from 3Hz to 1Hz over the three hours (left 145.33Hz to 146.33Hz, right 148.33Hz to 147.33Hz) — deep within the delta range. Stereo headphones keep the channels separate; the music can also be enjoyed on speakers. "3Hz" and "1Hz" describe the audio signal only. They are not measurements of brain activity or a promise of medical, therapeutic, sleep, or other health effects.

The soundtrack is generated as one continuous three-hour timeline rather than a short loop repeated: chord lengths are irregular, the tonal centre drifts over tens of minutes, and register, density and loudness all move on curves that never repeat. The music grows sparser toward dawn. Five still scenes are joined by slow ten-second dissolves. The platform, the shelter, the cedars and the lamp stay exactly where they are; only the light and the weather change. The final fifteen seconds contain the end card.

The images show a fictional Japanese railway station, not footage of a real place. Instrument-like sounds are synthesized, not live performance recordings.

初冬の夜、雪深い山あいの単線にある小さな無人駅。暮れ方の雪から、灯のともる頃、雪の止んだ夜の静けさ、夜更けの雪明かりを経て、夜明け前の青へ。灯りはホームの灯りひとつと、待合室の小さな窓だけです。Waves of Japanの第11作。

柔らかなパッド、フェルトピアノ風の音、静かなプラック、丸い点景音を組み合わせたオリジナルのアンビエントBGMです。点で鳴る音は、陰音階（ミ・ファ・ラ・シ・レ）の5音だけを使っています。Sunoや第三者の楽曲・サンプル音源は使わず、独自のパラメータからローカルで合成しました。音声に列車の音や環境音は入っていません。

ステレオ音声には146.83Hz（D3）付近の成分を左右に含みます。左右の周波数差は3時間かけて3Hzから1Hzへゆっくり下がります（左145.33→146.33Hz／右148.33→147.33Hz）。デルタ波の帯域の中で、さらに深く降りていきます。左右の音を別々の耳に届けるにはステレオヘッドホンをご使用ください。スピーカーでもアンビエント音楽として楽しめます。「3Hz」「1Hz」は音声信号の仕様であり、聴き手の脳波を測定した値や、医療・治療・睡眠などへの効果を保証するものではありません。
