An ancient walled hill city of the Mediterranean, like old Syracuse in Sicily, at night after sunset. Narrow stone streets climb between pale limestone houses with flat roofs and small arched doorways; every window is dark and shuttered, and the streets are silent and empty. On the hilltop stands a stern royal castle of heavy stone with a few small torches burning on its walls, the warmest spots in the image. Above, a deep indigo sky with a thin new moon and quiet stars. Colours: deep indigo, pale grey limestone, small orange torchlight. Lonely and hushed, a little uneasy. No people, no figures, no silhouettes of people, no faces, no hands, no animals except those named above, no cats. Japanese shin-hanga woodblock print in the manner of the 1920s-30s revival: flat layered areas of colour, visible woodgrain in the flat passages, softly gradated sky (bokashi), Western perspective and cast light held inside Japanese printmaking. Everything in the scene is drawn as part of the print, in the same flat woodblock manner, never photographic. Calm, dreamlike and hushed. Strong clear difference between the light and dark masses so the image still reads when reduced to a thumbnail. No text, no signature, no seal stamp, no lettering of any kind. No modern objects: no power lines, no cars, no trucks, no plastic, no electric signs. Wide 16:9 landscape. Exposed so that texture is clearly visible everywhere. Shadows are lifted and open, full of visible detail, nothing crushed to pure black. High dynamic range, rich texture even in the darkest areas.
