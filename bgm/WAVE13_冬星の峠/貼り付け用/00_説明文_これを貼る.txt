A small closed tea house at the top of an old mountain pass in rural Japan, on a clear early-winter night: from the last pale light at dusk, through the hour the first stars come out, to the crisp winter sky at its darkest and the hoarfrost of the small hours, and on to the quiet pass before dawn as the fainter stars fade. One paper lantern at the eave is the only light. The thirteenth volume of Waves of Japan.

This three-hour original ambient piece combines soft pads, felt-piano-like notes, quiet plucked tones, and small rounded points of sound. The melodic notes are drawn only from five tones (G B C D E). The music was synthesized locally from original parameters without Suno, third-party songs, or sampled recordings. There are no wind or environmental sounds in the audio.

A subtle stereo layer holds a tone in each channel around 196Hz (G3). The channel difference descends slowly from 8Hz to 4Hz over the three hours (left 192Hz to 194Hz, right 200Hz to 198Hz) — within the theta range. Stereo headphones keep the channels separate; the music can also be enjoyed on speakers. "8Hz" and "4Hz" describe the audio signal only. They are not measurements of brain activity or a promise of medical, therapeutic, sleep, or other health effects.

The soundtrack is generated as one continuous three-hour timeline rather than a short loop repeated: chord lengths are irregular, the tonal centre drifts over tens of minutes, and register, density and loudness all move on curves that never repeat. The music grows sparser toward dawn. Five still scenes are joined by slow ten-second dissolves. The tea house, the lantern and the stars stay exactly where they are; only the light and the frost change. The final fifteen seconds contain the end card.

The images show a fictional Japanese mountain pass, not footage of a real place. Instrument-like sounds are synthesized, not live performance recordings.

初冬の晴れた夜、古い峠道の頂にある、戸を閉めた小さな茶屋。暮れ方の淡い光から、最初の星が出る頃、いちばん暗く澄んだ冬の星空、霜の降りる夜更けを経て、淡い星が消えていく夜明け前の峠へ。灯りは軒先の提灯ひとつだけです。Waves of Japanの第13作。

柔らかなパッド、フェルトピアノ風の音、静かなプラック、丸い点景音を組み合わせたオリジナルのアンビエントBGMです。点で鳴る音は、ソ・シ・ド・レ・ミの5音だけを使っています。Sunoや第三者の楽曲・サンプル音源は使わず、独自のパラメータからローカルで合成しました。音声に風の音や環境音は入っていません。

ステレオ音声には196Hz（G3）付近の成分を左右に含みます。左右の周波数差は3時間かけて8Hzから4Hzへゆっくり下がります（左192→194Hz／右200→198Hz）。シータ波の帯域の中を降りていきます。左右の音を別々の耳に届けるにはステレオヘッドホンをご使用ください。スピーカーでもアンビエント音楽として楽しめます。「8Hz」「4Hz」は音声信号の仕様であり、聴き手の脳波を測定した値や、医療・治療・睡眠などへの効果を保証するものではありません。
