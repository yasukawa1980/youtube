## Title

Anubis: Judge of the Dead, or Protector? | What Museum Objects Reveal

## Description

Anubis, the canine-headed god of ancient Egypt, is famous for weighing the heart of the dead. But did he really judge alone? Using public collections and curatorial notes from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, this episode follows what Anubis does in the sources: guarding tombs, protecting the body, and overseeing the scale.

Museum photographs, modern facsimiles, explanatory diagrams, and AI-generated reconstructions are labeled separately. Religious beliefs are presented as beliefs, not historical events. Music: an original ambient score, not a reconstruction of ancient music.

## Title

アヌビス — 死者を裁く神か、守る神か

## Description

犬科の頭を持つ古代エジプトの神アヌビス。心臓を量る姿だけでなく、墓や身体を守る役割を、メトロポリタン美術館の公開資料から読み解きます。

博物館の資料写真、現代の壁画模写、図解、AI再現画を区別して表示しています。神話・信仰を現実に起きた出来事として断定しません。音楽は本作品用のオリジナル合成音です。
