An old ferry crossing on a wide, slow river in rural Japan, on a late-autumn night: from the last pale light at dusk, through the hour the lantern comes into its own and the first mist lifting from the water, to the deep layers of river mist at midnight and the still blue water before dawn. One paper lantern is the only light, and one empty boat waits at the jetty. The twelfth volume of Waves of Japan.

This three-hour original ambient piece combines soft pads, felt-piano-like notes, quiet plucked tones, and small rounded points of sound. The melodic notes are drawn only from five tones (E G A B C). The music was synthesized locally from original parameters without Suno, third-party songs, or sampled recordings. There are no water or environmental sounds in the audio.

A subtle stereo layer holds a tone in each channel around 164.81Hz (E3). The channel difference descends slowly from 6Hz to 2Hz over the three hours (left 161.81Hz to 163.81Hz, right 167.81Hz to 165.81Hz) — from the theta range into the delta range. Stereo headphones keep the channels separate; the music can also be enjoyed on speakers. "6Hz" and "2Hz" describe the audio signal only. They are not measurements of brain activity or a promise of medical, therapeutic, sleep, or other health effects.

The soundtrack is generated as one continuous three-hour timeline rather than a short loop repeated: chord lengths are irregular, the tonal centre drifts over tens of minutes, and register, density and loudness all move on curves that never repeat. The music grows sparser toward dawn. Five still scenes are joined by slow ten-second dissolves. The jetty, the boat and the lantern stay exactly where they are; only the light and the mist change. The final fifteen seconds contain the end card.

The images show a fictional Japanese river crossing, not footage of a real place. Instrument-like sounds are synthesized, not live performance recordings.

晩秋の夜、ゆったりと流れる広い川の古い渡し場。暮れ方の淡い光から、提灯の灯りが満ちる頃、水面に霧が立ちはじめる夜、夜更けの深い川霧を経て、夜明け前の静かな水面へ。灯りは提灯ひとつだけで、桟橋には誰も乗っていない舟が一艘つながれています。Waves of Japanの第12作。

柔らかなパッド、フェルトピアノ風の音、静かなプラック、丸い点景音を組み合わせたオリジナルのアンビエントBGMです。点で鳴る音は、ミ・ソ・ラ・シ・ドの5音だけを使っています。Sunoや第三者の楽曲・サンプル音源は使わず、独自のパラメータからローカルで合成しました。音声に水の音や環境音は入っていません。

ステレオ音声には164.81Hz（E3）付近の成分を左右に含みます。左右の周波数差は3時間かけて6Hzから2Hzへゆっくり下がります（左161.81→163.81Hz／右167.81→165.81Hz）。シータ波の帯域からデルタ波の帯域へ降りていきます。左右の音を別々の耳に届けるにはステレオヘッドホンをご使用ください。スピーカーでもアンビエント音楽として楽しめます。「6Hz」「2Hz」は音声信号の仕様であり、聴き手の脳波を測定した値や、医療・治療・睡眠などへの効果を保証するものではありません。
