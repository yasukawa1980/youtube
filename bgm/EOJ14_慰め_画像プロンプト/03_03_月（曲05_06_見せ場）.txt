The same old grove of wild camellia trees under a full moon: the most beautiful scene of the night. The grove is an old wood of wild Japanese camellia trees (yabu-tsubaki, Camellia japonica) on a quiet hillside in Japan: smooth pale grey trunks, dense dark glossy evergreen leaves, and single deep-red five-petalled flowers, each with a short cup of golden stamens, not roses, not peonies, not double flowers. A narrow earthen path covered in soft green moss winds through the grove, and whole fallen camellia flowers lie face-up on the moss, still perfectly red and intact. The trees close in on both sides and overhead, which fills the background and shuts out any further view: no distant mountains, no sea, no lake, no far horizon.
