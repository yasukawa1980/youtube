## Title

Krampus: Is He Really Older Than Christmas?

## Description

Is Krampus really older than Christmas? We follow a bishop's legend, devils in Saint Nicholas customs, colorful postcards, and nineteenth-century ideas about a pagan past.

Narration uses synthetic speech. Artworks, postcards and photographs are labelled with their dates and sources. Book scans were used for textual research only.
