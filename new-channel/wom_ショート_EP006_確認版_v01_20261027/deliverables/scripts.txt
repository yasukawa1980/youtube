EP006 S1 — The riddle isn't in the play（なぞの言葉は、劇に出てこない）
Sophocles' play Oedipus the King remembers the riddling Sphinx, but it never gives the words of the riddle. Later writers do: what has one voice, and becomes four-footed, two-footed, and three-footed? A human being. In these texts, there is no morning, noon, or evening.

EP006 S2 — The Great Sphinx isn't a woman（ギザのスフィンクスは、女ではない）
The Great Sphinx of Giza is not a woman. It has a lion's body and the face of a king, in the royal headcloth of a pharaoh. Hatshepsut, a woman who ruled as pharaoh, appears as a sphinx too, with a king's ceremonial beard.

EP006 S3 — The nose was gone before Napoleon（鼻は、ナポレオンより前に欠けていた）
It is often said that Napoleon's soldiers shot off the Sphinx's nose. Yet drawings by the Danish traveler Frederic Norden, who visited in the 1730s, already show the face without its nose. They were printed long before Napoleon came to Egypt in 1798.
