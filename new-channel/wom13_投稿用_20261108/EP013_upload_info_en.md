Is The Night Watch Really Set at Night?

Is Rembrandt's The Night Watch really a scene at night? Follow the painting from the civic guard hall it was made for, through early descriptions and the cutting of its edges, to the name recorded long after Rembrandt's lifetime. Historical texts and museum collections help distinguish a later name from the scene on the canvas.
