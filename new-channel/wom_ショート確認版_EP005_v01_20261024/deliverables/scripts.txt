EP005 T1 — The first tengu was a star（最初の天狗は、星だった）
In the Nihon Shoki, a chronicle of 720, a great star crosses the sky in 637 with a loud sound. A monk named Bin says it is not a falling star. It is a tengu, a celestial dog. Its barking sounds like thunder.

EP005 T2 — A Buddha in a persimmon tree（柿の木の仏）
In a tale from the twelfth century, a Buddha appears in a persimmon tree, shining and scattering flowers. One minister just keeps staring. At last the Buddha vanishes, and a great kite with a broken wing falls from the tree.

EP005 T3 — The bird never went away（鳥の姿は消えなかった）
Long-nosed tengu came later, but the bird never went away. In Hokusai's sketchbook, first published in 1815, the figure labeled tengu is still a great bird. For centuries, the two forms lived side by side.
