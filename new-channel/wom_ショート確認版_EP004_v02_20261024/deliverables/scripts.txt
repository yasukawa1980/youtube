EP004 P1 — Pandora's box was a jar（箱ではなく、壺だった）
Pandora never opened a box. In Hesiod's poem, from around 700 BCE, she lifts the great lid of a jar. The Greek word is pithos, a storage jar like this one, made on Crete, about one and a half meters tall.

EP004 P2 — How the jar became a box（壺が箱になったのは）
So where did Pandora's box come from? The change is usually traced to Erasmus. In his book of proverbs, printed in Venice in 1508, Hesiod's pithos, a great jar, became a pyxis, a small box. The word stuck.

EP004 P3 — Why did hope stay?（なぜ希望だけが残ったのか）
When Pandora lifted the lid, countless troubles spread among humankind. Only hope stayed inside, beneath the rim of the jar. The lid stopped it, by the will of Zeus. Was hope kept safe for us, or kept away from us? Hesiod does not say.
