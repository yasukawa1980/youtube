# EP007 投稿情報・下書き

## Title

The First Thanksgiving: What the 1621 Letter Actually Says

## Description

What does the surviving letter actually say about the 1621 harvest feast? We follow the printed record, the later name, the November date and the painting that shaped a familiar image.

Synthetic narration and clearly labelled AI reconstructions are used. AI scenes are illustrations, not historical evidence or precise architectural reconstructions. The opening table shows a familiar later image, not the documented menu of 1621.
