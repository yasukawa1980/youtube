K1 — Not found in the records（呪いの言葉は墓の記録に見つからない）
The famous curse on Tutankhamun's tomb? In the records we checked, that warning does not appear as an inscription from the tomb. What the tomb does hold are wishes for the king himself. One alabaster cup wishes him millions of years of life.

K2 — A death, then a curse（死、そして呪い）
When Lord Carnarvon died in Cairo in April 1923, newspapers around the world linked his death to Tutankhamun's tomb. They quoted Arthur Conan Doyle on a spirit-like force guarding it. On the same pages, other experts disagreed. The curse story grew from pages like these.

K3 — Older than the discovery（墓の発見より古い呪い）
The mummy's curse is older than the discovery of Tutankhamun's tomb. In 1869, Louisa May Alcott, the author of Little Women, published a story called "Lost in a Pyramid; or, The Mummy's Curse." That was more than fifty years before Howard Carter found the tomb.
