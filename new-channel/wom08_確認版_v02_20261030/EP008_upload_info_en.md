## Title

The Curse of Tutankhamun: Where Did It Come From?

## Description

Where did Tutankhamun’s curse come from? We follow the 1923 excavation account, contemporary newspaper debates and earlier fiction, and distinguish reported legends from the words recorded in the tomb.

Synthetic narration is used. Only the candle scene in P14 is AI-generated (Kling AI); it does not recreate a historical event. Archival photos, newspaper columns and printed texts are labelled with their sources. The historical cohort study found no statistically significant association; its sample was small and records incomplete.

The Huy scene is a 1923–27 facsimile from another tomb. Color objects are contemporary examples, not KV62 finds. Perneb is a different chapel from the one quoted. Archival photographs use a single warm tone.
