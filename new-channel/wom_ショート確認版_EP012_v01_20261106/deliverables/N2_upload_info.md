## Title

Santa Once Wore the Flag

## Description

In January eighteen sixty-three, during the American Civil War, Thomas Nast drew Santa Claus for Harper's Weekly. His Santa visited a Union army camp, in a coat of stars and striped trousers. It was printed in black and white.
