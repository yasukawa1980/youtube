## Title

Could You Fail the Weighing of the Heart?

## Description

Could you fail the Weighing of the Heart? We read the Book of the Dead through museum papyri, a list of wrongs never done, forty-two gods, and a spell addressed to the heart.

Narration uses synthetic speech. Museum objects are labelled with their names, dates and sources. The English quotations follow Budge (1895); the denials draw on different versions. Modern research is listed separately. Book scans are not used as video images.
