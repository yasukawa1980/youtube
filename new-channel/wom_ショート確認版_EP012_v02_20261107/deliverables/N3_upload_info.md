## Title

In 1821, Santa Had One Reindeer

## Description

In eighteen twelve, Washington Irving pictured Saint Nicholas riding over the tops of the trees in a wagon. In eighteen twenty-one, a little book showed his sleigh pulled by a single reindeer. Two years later, a poem gave him eight tiny reindeer.
