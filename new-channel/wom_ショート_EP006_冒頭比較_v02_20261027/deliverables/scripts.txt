EP006 S1 — なぞの言葉は劇で語られない
The Sphinx's riddle is never quoted in Sophocles' Oedipus the King. Later writers give it: what has one voice, and becomes four-footed, two-footed, and three-footed? A human being. In these texts, there is no morning, noon, or evening.

EP006 S2 — ギザのスフィンクスは女ではない（変更なし）
The Great Sphinx of Giza is not a woman. It has a lion's body and the face of a king, in the royal headcloth of a pharaoh. Hatshepsut, a woman who ruled as pharaoh, appears as a sphinx too, with a king's ceremonial beard.

EP006 S3 — 鼻はナポレオンより前から欠けていた
The Sphinx's nose was already gone before Napoleon. Drawings by the Danish traveler Frederic Norden, who visited in the 1730s, already show the face without its nose. They were printed long before Napoleon came to Egypt in 1798.
