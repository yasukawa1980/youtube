## 動きの比較

**全37本編ショットが、p95÷中央値 ≤2.5 を満たした。基準を超えるショットは0件。**

方法：Every decoded frame at 30fps, grayscale 8-bit foreground ROI. Mean absolute pixel difference between consecutive frames. Interior 70% of largest foreground image; diagrams use x200,y200,w1520,h480; H02 pan uses x260,y180,w1400,h350. No downsampling, temporal filtering, dropped samples or percentile trimming. Same algorithm for v01/v02; layout-specific ROI follows the enlarged artwork.
