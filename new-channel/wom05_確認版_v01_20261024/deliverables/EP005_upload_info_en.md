## Title

The First Tengu Was a Star | What the Oldest Sources Say

## Description

The oldest Japanese tengu is a star. Follow the word from the Nihon Shoki through medieval bird tales to long-nosed masks, carvings and prints. The bird form remained alongside the later long-nosed figure.

Synthetic narration and labelled AI illustrations are used. AI scenes are atmospheric illustrations, not historical evidence or exact reconstructions of real sacred sites.
