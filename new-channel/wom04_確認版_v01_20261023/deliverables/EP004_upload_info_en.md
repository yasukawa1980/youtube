## Title

Pandora's Box Was Never a Box | What the Ancient Sources Say

## Description

Pandora's familiar box began as a large storage jar. Follow the vessels through Hesiod, a fable by Babrius, Erasmus's printed book, and later paintings. The sources tell different stories about what was inside.

This video uses synthetic narration. Labelled AI reconstructions illustrate atmosphere; they are not historical evidence or exact reconstructions of real sites. Modern interpretations are distinguished from ancient accounts.
