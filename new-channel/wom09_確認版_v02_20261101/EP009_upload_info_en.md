## Title

The Trojan Horse: Is It in the Iliad?

## Description

Is the Trojan horse in the Iliad? We follow a funeral, a song at a feast, lost Greek poems, an ancient image, and Virgil's Aeneid to see how the famous story reached us.

Narration uses synthetic speech. Works of art and photographs are labelled with their dates and sources; later works are identified as later imaginings. Printed book scans are research references only and do not appear in the video.
